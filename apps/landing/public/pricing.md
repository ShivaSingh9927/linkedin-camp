# Pricing — Qampi

Qampi is an AI LinkedIn + cold-email outreach platform. Two price books: Global (USD) and India (INR). Annual billing = 2 months free (monthly-equivalent shown in parentheses). Every account starts on Free; no credit card required; cancel anytime. Sign up: https://app.qampi.com/register

## Free
- Price: $0 / ₹0 (forever)
- LinkedIn invites: 20 / week
- Leads stored: 100
- Email-finder credits: 10 (one-time)
- AI-written messages, inbox sync + reply
- Templates: 4–5 starter templates
- Copilot: lead search
- Support: community

## Core
- Price: $19/month ($16/month billed annually, $190/year) · ₹399/month (₹333/month billed annually, ₹3,990/year)
- LinkedIn invites: 300 / month
- Leads stored: 1,500
- Email-finder credits: 100 / month
- Templates: all 43
- Copilot: full
- CRM sync: HubSpot, Pipedrive, Notion
- Support: email (48h)

## Pro (most popular)
- Price: $49/month ($41/month billed annually, $490/year) · ₹1,199/month (₹999/month billed annually, ₹11,990/year)
- LinkedIn invites: 800 / month
- Leads stored: 2,500
- Email-finder credits: 300 / month
- Everything in Core
- Public API + webhooks (Zapier, Make, n8n)
- Support: priority (4h)

## Business
- Price: $69/month ($58/month billed annually, $690/year) · ₹1,699/month (₹1,416/month billed annually, ₹16,990/year)
- LinkedIn invites: 800 / month
- Leads stored: 5,000
- Email-finder credits: 500 / month
- Everything in Pro
- Cold email + multichannel (LinkedIn + email) campaigns
- Team workspace, up to 25 seats (extra seats are paid add-ons)
- Support: priority (4h)

## Notes
- Every plan, Free included, gets the same account protection: a dedicated proxy per LinkedIn account, human-like pacing, working-hours sending, and a hard safety ceiling of 18 invites and 40 messages per day.
